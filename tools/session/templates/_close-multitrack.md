To end a session whose OTHER tracks belong to dead instances, you may seal them as ABANDONED: `tpm session close --session <NNNN> --track <letter>` (their record is left alone, no handoff is written for them, who/when is recorded in a seal marker). **Never do this on your own.** First run it WITHOUT `--even-if-running`: it writes nothing, prints what it would seal (track, last write, liveness) and exits `4`. Show the person that evidence in plain words (a track whose liveness says `owner process running` may be a live colleague — say so) and ask a direct question; only on an **explicit yes** re-run with `--even-if-running`. If the person later wants such a track back, `tpm session unseal --session <NNNN> --track <letter>`. A track sealed by someone else makes its live owner's next `save`/`note`/`punchlist` refuse loudly (`ETRACK_SEALED`, naming who sealed it and when; nothing they saved is lost) - if YOU hit that refusal, tell the person and do not un-seal without their say-so.
